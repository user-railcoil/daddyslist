Sync light + backups

So you always know it's saving

1. A little light, bottom-leftGreen means saved. Orange means saving. Red means saving has stopped, so tell Daddy. Tap it to see the details.
2. Daily backupsEvery day the server keeps a copy of everything, for 14 days. Tap the light and press Export backup to save a copy to your phone or laptop. Daddy can also put an old day back.

Nothing was deleted. If the light turns red, reload once and tell Daddy. ❤

New ways to earn points

Your point potential is now way higher than 12 a day

1. Type what you eat: +¼ point eachWhen you tap Eat, type in everything you ate. Every item you type earns ¼ point, and your list is saved in History.
2. Dots on your tasksEvery task has a little column of dots in its top-right corner. A bright dot means you did it that day. A dim one means you missed it. Each task gets one new dot when the day ends, and after 7 it starts over at 1. Paused tasks get a dot too. You won't see any until today is over.
3. 7 in a row = +3 pointsGet all 7 dots bright on a task and you earn 3 extra points for it.
4. One task changedThe second Hydrate is now Clean for 5 minutes. Do a quick 5 minute clean and check it off.
5. New rewardsSkip a day costs 3 points: take a whole day off with no missed-task penalty and your dots stay put. There's also a new $35 reward for 300 points. Each reward can only be claimed once.

Keep those points coming. ❤
